Connect AI agents to the real world — instantly.

mcp.build hosts open-source MCP servers so your agent can take real action — send emails, call APIs, and more — with nothing to deploy and nothing to configure. Copy an endpoint, connect it in seconds, and give your agent new superpowers.

Why mcp.build

  • Hosted and ready

    hosted

    Every MCP is already running. No servers, no infra — just a URL you paste and go.

  • Built for plain language

    agent-friendly

    MCPs on mcp.build are designed to take natural-language prompts and turn them into the right tool calls. No rigid schemas, no boilerplate.

  • Open source

    community

    Every MCP is open source. Inspect the code, self-host it, or contribute your own.
